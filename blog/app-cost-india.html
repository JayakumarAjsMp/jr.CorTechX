How Much Does an App Cost in India? (2025 Guide)

Planning to build a mobile app? Here's a transparent, no-fluff breakdown of what it actually costs — from a simple MVP to a full-featured product — with real numbers for the Indian market.

JR
jr.CorTechX Team Software Development · Tirupathur, Tamilnadu, India

One of the most common questions we get is: "How much will my app cost?" The honest answer is — it depends. But that's not helpful, so let's break it down properly.

The Short Answer

  • Simple MVP app: ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000
  • Mid-range app with backend: ₹1,50,000 – ₹5,00,000
  • Complex / enterprise app: ₹5,00,000 – ₹20,00,000+

These are realistic Indian market rates for a quality development partner — not the cheapest freelancer on Fiverr, and not a large agency charging MNC rates.

What Factors Drive the Cost?

1. Platform — Android, iOS, or Both?

Building for one platform (Android or iOS) is cheaper than both. Cross-platform frameworks like Flutter let you build for both simultaneously, which reduces cost by 30–40% compared to building two separate native apps.

3. Backend — Do You Need One?

Many apps need a server to store data, handle user accounts, and run business logic. A backend adds significant cost but is essential for most real-world apps.

  • No backend (local/offline app): Cheapest — no server costs
  • Firebase (BaaS): Quick to set up, good for MVPs — adds ₹20k–₹60k in dev time
  • Custom backend (Kotlin/Node.js + PostgreSQL): Most scalable — adds ₹80k–₹3L depending on complexity

4. UI/UX Design

A well-designed app costs more upfront but converts better and retains users longer. Basic UI design is usually included in development quotes. Custom design with animations and brand identity adds ₹30k–₹1.5L.

Hidden Costs to Budget For

  • Google Play Store: $25 one-time (~₹2,100)
  • Apple App Store: $99/year (~₹8,300/year)
  • Server hosting: ₹500–₹5,000/month depending on traffic
  • SSL certificate: Usually free with modern hosting
  • Maintenance & updates: Budget 15–20% of build cost per year
  • Third-party APIs: Maps, SMS, payment gateways have usage fees

How to Reduce Your App Development Cost

  1. Start with an MVP — build only the core features first. Validate with real users before adding more.
  2. Use cross-platform (Flutter) — one codebase for Android + iOS saves 30–40%.
  3. Have a clear spec — vague requirements lead to scope creep and cost overruns. Write down exactly what you need.
  4. Choose the right partner — a boutique agency gives you senior-level work at a fraction of large agency prices.
  5. Avoid over-engineering early — you don't need microservices and Kubernetes for your first 1,000 users.
